Matrix 0.1.2 for all round 480 × 480 devices in the checked-in Zeus catalog.

- Expands installation packages from three Balance 2 XT variants to the full 40-deviceSource catalog.
- Retains Orbitron labels, uniform 2 × 2 pixel dot-matrix digits, native indicators and AOD.
- GTR 3 Pro variants use system-font labels and dates because their original OS does not support custom TTF fonts. Bitmap time and activity digits retain the Matrix style.

Download the ZIP matching your deviceSource and open it with Gadgetbridge. Package names include the device model and deviceSource.

Balance 2 XT was previously tried by the user. Other models and the new legacy font fallback are build/test-validated only; installation, firmware-specific rendering and AOD require physical-device checks.

Orbitron is bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, included in every installation ZIP. Version 0.1.1 remains available unchanged.
