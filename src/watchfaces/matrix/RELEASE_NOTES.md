Matrix 0.1.1 for Amazfit Balance 2 XT.

- Orbitron Medium replaces raster label lettering for labels, localized date and percent sign.
- Uniform 2 × 2 pixel LEDs: 15 × 21 cells for the time digits and 5 × 7 for small values.
- Native Zepp arcs replace baked dial outlines. The heart-rate ring is masked to an upper semicircle.
- Existing seconds dial alignment, native activity values and minimal AOD are preserved.

The user approved the Orbitron visual variant after it was compiled and loaded in the Balance 2 simulator. The 0.1.1 installation ZIP was prepared for device testing; explicit confirmation of physical-device checks is pending. Matrix 0.1.0 was previously tried successfully on Balance 2 XT with deviceSource `10486017`.

Download the ZIP matching your deviceSource (`10486016`, `10486017` or `10486019`) and open it with Gadgetbridge. Check custom-font rendering, percent-sign spacing, dial outlines, heart-rate semicircle, seconds rollover and normal/AOD transitions.
